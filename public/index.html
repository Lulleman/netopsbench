LOOKUP
MAC Vendor Lookup
Find the registered vendor for a MAC address or OUI. Supports colon, dash, dot and plain formats.






Examples: 00:1A:2B:3C:4D:5E, 001A.2B3C.4D5E, 00-1A-2B or 001A2B.




Vendor registration data is retrieved through our lookup service. A MAC address you submit is sent to that service to perform the lookup.
